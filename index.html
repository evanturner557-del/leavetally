Common questions

Does this work for irregular hours?

No. Irregular-hours and part-year workers have separate rules. See GOV.UK's holiday entitlement guidance.

Is it legal advice?

No. It applies the basic statutory rule. Contracts can give more.

Is my data stored?

No. The calculation happens in your browser.
